Performance descriptors

Excellent (85–100% of a criterion): Meets requirements thoroughly, communicates clearly, and demonstrates independent work.

Good (70–84%): Completes most requirements with minor weaknesses.

Developing (50–69%): Shows partial understanding; needs clearer evidence or language.

Needs improvement (below 50%): Missing major components, limited contribution, or insufficient evidence.

Group progress reports also document each member’s speaking role, product contribution, rehearsals and progress. They provide evidence for the Teamwork and AI Use criteria, but they do not add extra points beyond the 10-point rubric.

Student & Lecturer Guidelines

🧠 Responsible AI Use

AI may help with ideas, research questions, language checks, visuals, and editing. Students must understand their work, verify facts and budgets, and credit any AI tools used.

Do not: submit AI-created content you cannot explain, invent survey responses, or use unverified claims.

📅 Recommended Timeline

Week 1: Choose a topic, assign roles, plan tasks, research, collect evidence, and submit the first report.

Week 2: Produce the deliverables, review English, rehearse, get feedback, submit the final report, and rehearse a 30-minute presentation with everyone speaking.

✅ Submission Checklist

Original image/video/handmade product; bullet-point slides; 30-minute team presentation; speaking role for every member; rehearsal plan; sources and AI-use acknowledgements; evidence links; and two weekly group progress reports.

🎤 Mandatory Presentation Policy

Time: 30 minutes per group, including a demonstration and audience questions. Suggested division: 3-minute introduction, 17-minute explanation, 5-minute product showcase and 5-minute Q&A.

Slides: Short bullet points and visual examples; no text-heavy paragraphs. Use the slides as speaking support, not as a script.

Participation: Every group member must speak in English. Divide speaking time clearly, and make individual contributions visible in the progress report.

Creative product: At least one original visual, video, handmade or physical product must be demonstrated. Multiple formats are welcome.

Progress reports: Submit Week 1 and Week 2 updates, track responsibility, report evidence and problems, and record each member’s speaking part and final product contribution.

✍️ Sample brainstorming prompt

I am a university student learning English at A2 level. My project topic is [TOPIC]. Help my group brainstorm five creative ideas. Use simple English and practical examples. Do not complete our project for us. Ask us three questions to develop our own ideas.
